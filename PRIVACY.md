\## 1. Overview

StreamCtx has two very different data situations depending on which tier you

use:



\### Self-hosted / open-source SDK (Free)

\- All data (sessions, checkpoints, context, attribution scores) is stored in

&#x20; \*\*your own\*\* SQLite database or your own Supabase instance.

\- StreamCtx (the company) has no access to this data, does not collect it,

&#x20; and cannot see it. This tier is effectively "your infrastructure, your

&#x20; data."



\### Hosted / Pro tiers

\- We store session metadata, checkpoints, and usage data on infrastructure we

&#x20; manage (currently Supabase) so we can provide the dashboard, managed

&#x20; hosting, and team access features.

\- We do not sell your data to third parties.

\- We do not use your session content to train models.



\## 2. What we collect (Hosted/Pro only)

\- \*\*Account info:\*\* name, email, billing details (processed by our payment

&#x20; provider, not stored directly by us).

\- \*\*Usage data:\*\* API call counts, token usage, error rates — used for

&#x20; billing and for showing you your own dashboard stats.

\- \*\*Session/context data:\*\* the actual agent session content you send through

&#x20; the Hosted SDK, so checkpoint/resume and attribution features work.



\## 3. How we use it

\- To provide the service you're paying for (checkpointing, dashboards,

&#x20; attribution scoring).

\- To debug issues you report (with your permission, on a case-by-case basis).

\- To improve the product (aggregated, anonymized usage patterns only — never

&#x20; your raw session content).
